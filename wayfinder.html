      Settings

      Please read

      Wayfinder points you toward a pin or address in a straight line. It does not know about streets, curbs, crossings, or traffic, and GPS can be off by 10 meters or more. Keep using your cane or guide dog and listen for traffic. This is a prototype, not a replacement for a navigation aid you already trust.

      Apple Maps and Google Maps know about streets and crossings. Use them for the route, then come back to Wayfinder when you are close, to be guided the last few steps to an exact pin such as a door.

      Keep the screen on and this page open while you walk. Hold the phone flat, pointing the way you face, for the best compass reading.
